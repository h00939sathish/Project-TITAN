# Hypothesis: Long-term trend following with MA(50,200) crossover

**id:** 2026-07-13-ma-50-200
**title:** A 50/200-day moving average crossover on SPY generates positive risk-adjusted returns after costs, with sufficient OOS trades for statistical confidence
**economic_rationale:** Long-term trends (200-day MA) capture sustained market moves like the 2020 COVID recovery and 2023-2024 uptrend, while the 50-day crossover reduces whipsaws compared to shorter MAs. The 200-day MA is a widely followed proxy for the primary trend. This is a structural, not predictive, strategy — it follows, not forecasts.
**strategy_params:** fast=50, slow=200
**instrument:** SPY
**universe:** SPY only
**calendar:** 2020-01-02 to 2024-12-31
**train_period:** 2020-01-02
**test_period:** 2023-01-01
**strategy_id:** ma-crossover
**expected_trade_frequency:** ~2 trades/year (quarterly MA crossover)
**sample_adequacy_policy:** Path A
**path_b_evidence_standard:**
**success_criteria:**
- Sharpe > 0.5 OOS
- Max drawdown < SPY benchmark max drawdown OOS
- Win rate > 35% OOS
- Minimum 30 OOS trades (gate)
**failure_criteria:**
- Sharpe < 0 OOS
- Strategy loses to buy-and-hold on risk-adjusted basis OOS
- Fewer than 30 OOS trades without documented override reason
**costs:** 1.0 bps commission, 0.5 bps slippage; CA-adjusted dividends via common_adjustments()
**notes:** Preregistered before any OOS results are known. No parameter tuning based on 2023-2024 MA(5,20) results. The 50/200 parameters are chosen for their economic motivation (primary trend proxy + entry filter), not for backtest optimization.
